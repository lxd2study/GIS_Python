## Style Prompt

Project-native remote sensing product film. The visual language comes from the Vue application: pale laboratory canvas, crisp white operational panels, satellite-map texture, teal status accents, amber warning markers, and dense but readable engineering UI. The tone is technical, quiet, and credible rather than cinematic fantasy.

## Colors

- `#f3f5f4` canvas: inherited app background.
- `#ffffff` surface: cards and operational panels.
- `#1f2b27` ink: primary Chinese text.
- `#5f726b` muted: secondary labels and metadata.
- `#0f7c66` primary teal: active processing, AOI, health status.
- `#8a5c12` amber: warnings and product-level differentiation.
- `#b23a32` red: failed states and QA alerts.

## Typography

- Display: `League Gothic`, uppercase project labels and numeric callouts, chosen as a deterministic HyperFrames-renderable substitute for the app's condensed Teko heading voice.
- Chinese/UI: `Noto Sans Japanese`, `sans-serif` for CJK-safe readable narration in the render pipeline.
- Data: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace for scene IDs, formulas, and file paths.

## Motion

- Medium energy, operational product pacing.
- Use directional wipes, focus sweeps, and panel builds. Avoid chaotic glitches.
- Data and UI elements should enter by sliding, scaling, or drawing in, as if a workflow is becoming executable.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use neon sci-fi palettes or generic blue-purple gradients.
- Do not make the product feel like a landing page; show working panels, maps, tasks, and outputs.
- Do not hide the Chinese project name behind tiny UI chrome.
- Do not use soft decorative blobs or irrelevant stock imagery.
