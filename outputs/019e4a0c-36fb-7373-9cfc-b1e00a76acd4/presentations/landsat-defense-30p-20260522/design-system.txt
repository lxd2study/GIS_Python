Design direction

Tone: official school defense, steady and formal.
Canvas: 16:9, 1280 x 720.
Palette: white paper, academic navy, muted blue, teal, restrained gold.
Chrome: top academic rule, small slide number, source footer, optional school emblem placeholder.
Rhythm: cover, flow/agenda, section dividers, diagrams, code evidence, screenshots, test close.
Evidence policy: real project screenshots and source-code facts only; no fabricated accuracy metrics.
